# Past and future anthropogenic impacts on Atlantic bluefin tuna through the eyes of demographic and adaptation analyses on range-wide whole genomes
This repository includes all the scripts used to conduct genomic analyses on the high-coverage sequences to explore demographic history, adaptation, and the impact of human activities on Atlantic bluefin tuna.
